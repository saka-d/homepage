Terms and Definitions

Computational Chemistry and Cheminformatics Glossary

An index of abbreviations and technical terms used across this website, linked to the corresponding chapters.

In this chapter

  1. A–F
  2. G–M
  3. N–S
  4. T–Z and symbols

1. A–F

AFIR
Artificial Force Induced Reaction, a method for exploring candidate reaction paths. GRRM
Cube
A format storing scalar fields on a three-dimensional grid with atomic coordinates. Cube
Dual descriptor
A conceptual-DFT descriptor comparing nucleophilic and electrophilic response. Psi4
EDA
Analysis partitioning an interaction energy under a stated definition. ORCA
ETKDG
Distance-geometry conformer generation with experimental torsional knowledge. RDKit

2. G–M

Fukui function
Local density response to a change in electron number.
IGMH
Independent Gradient Model based on Hirshfeld partitioning. Multiwfn
InChIKey
A fixed-length irreversible hash derived from InChI. Cheminformatics
IRC
Intrinsic reaction coordinate followed in both directions from a transition state. Gaussian
MMFF
An empirical force field widely used for organic molecules. RDKit

3. N–S

NCI
Noncovalent-interaction analysis based on low-density, low-gradient regions. NCIplot
PCA
Dimensionality reduction onto orthogonal high-variance directions. Data analysis
RDG
Reduced Density Gradient, a dimensionless quantity used in NCI analysis.
RMSD
Distance between two structures after atom correspondence and alignment. RDKit
SAPT
Perturbative decomposition of intermolecular interactions. Psi4
SCF
Self-consistent iteration of density and orbitals. Gaussian

4. T–Z and symbols

Sterimol
Substituent length and minimum/maximum widths about a defined axis. Steric descriptors
%Vbur
Fraction of a defined sphere occupied by van der Waals volume.
ΔΔG‡
Difference between competing activation free energies. Reaction selectivity
Voxel
One element of a three-dimensional grid and the basic unit of field features.
